Scramble-control scorer

A cofolding confidence score that ranks a peptide above an unrelated decoy may be responding to amino-acid composition rather than to binding. Scoring a candidate against permutations of itself holds composition and length fixed and varies only residue order.

Everything here runs in your browser. No structure is uploaded anywhere, and nothing is folded — folding needs a GPU. Bring predicted complexes with pLDDT in the B-factor column; Boltz, Chai-1 and AlphaFold all write it there.

1. Generate the permutations to fold

2. Score the folded structures

Readout is the paper's: 8 Å between representative atoms, CB where present and CA otherwise, mean CA pLDDT over the contacting residues of both sides. Panels and per-fold scores: scramble-control-panels — 2,456 folds, 16 inference arms, 75 receptors.
