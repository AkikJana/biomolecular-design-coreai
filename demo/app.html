—
Why scrambles? A permutation of a candidate has identical composition and length and differs only in order. A confidence score that cannot separate a peptide from its own scramble is reading composition, not binding — that was this project's central negative result. Each candidate is therefore reported as a z against its own permutations, not as a raw score.

The z divides by a spread pooled across every candidate in the job. A spread taken from one candidate's two or three permutations collapses whenever they land close together — an early build reported z = +77 that way, which is not a number to act on.
